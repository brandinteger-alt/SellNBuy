How It Works

List a Product

Complete Payment

Marketplace Rules

Brand Integer — Buy & Sell Rules

📹 UNBOXING VIDEO — IMPORTANT

For any defect, damage, shortage, tampering or wrong-product claim, the buyer must have a continuous unboxing video from the sealed package through the opening and product reveal.

1. Buyer payment

  • Buyer pays the product price upfront before the product is shipped.
  • Courier charges are paid separately by the buyer and calculated by Delhivery based on weight and distance, or lower as per buyer's discretion.

2. Seller price & bargaining

  • Seller provides a listed price and a minimum acceptable price.
  • Brand Integer may negotiate with buyers on the seller's behalf, but will not agree below the seller's minimum without approval.

3. Seller payment

  • Seller receives payment only after the buyer has received the product and approved it.

4. Product location & courier charges

  • Every listing displays the seller's listing location.
  • The displayed product price is exclusive of courier charges.
  • Courier charges are separate from the product price and will be charged as per the actual charges levied by Delhivery, our logistics partner, or lower as per buyer discretion.

5. Transit loss or destruction

  • If a product is lost or destroyed in transit, Brand Integer is not responsible for the loss.
  • The buyer will receive the applicable full refund and the seller will not receive the sale proceeds for that transaction.

6. Buyer claims

  • Defect, damage, shortage, tampering or wrong-product claims require a continuous unboxing video showing the package before opening through the product reveal.

7. Wrong or defective products

  • If a seller ships a wrong product or a defective product that does not match the photos or stated condition, the seller bears the full loss.
  • The buyer's unboxing video serves as proof. The seller will not receive payment or will be partially paid based on Brand Integer's assessment, and the buyer will be fully compensated.
  • To prevent disputes: sellers must clearly describe any flaws, defects, wear, stains or damage in the product description and include clear photos showing all imperfections.
  • Misrepresenting product condition is a violation and may result in account suspension.

8. Fraud protection

  • Brand Integer will use reasonable transaction controls to reduce fraudulent activity. No platform can guarantee that fraud is impossible, so users should provide accurate information and follow the transaction process.

9. Platform role

  • Brand Integer facilitates discovery, transaction coordination and payment release according to the stated process.
