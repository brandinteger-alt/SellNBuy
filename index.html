Marketplace Rules

Brand Integer — Buy & Sell Rules

📹 UNBOXING VIDEO — IMPORTANT

For any defect, damage, shortage, tampering or wrong-product claim, the buyer must have a continuous unboxing video from the sealed package through the opening and product reveal.

1. Buyer payment

  • A ₹5 platform fee is added at checkout.
  • Buyer pays the product price upfront before the product is shipped.
  • Courier charges are paid separately by the buyer and calculated by Delhivery based on weight and distance, or lower as per buyer's discretion.

2. Seller price

  • Seller provides a listed price and a minimum acceptable price.
  • Brand Integer manages buyer communication on the seller's behalf and will not sell below the seller's minimum without approval.

3. Seller payment

  • Brand Integer charges sellers a 4% commission, deducted from the payout.
  • Seller receives payment only after the buyer has received the product and approved it.

4. Product location & courier charges

  • Every listing displays the seller's city and pincode. The seller's phone number is never shown to buyers.
  • The displayed product price is exclusive of courier charges.
  • Courier charges are separate from the product price and will be charged as per the actual charges levied by Delhivery, our logistics partner, or lower as per buyer discretion.

5. Transit loss or destruction

  • If a product is lost or destroyed in transit, Brand Integer is not responsible for the loss.
  • The buyer will receive the applicable full refund and the seller will not receive the sale proceeds for that transaction.

6. Buyer claims

  • ⏱ 24-hour rule: Report any issue with your unboxing video within 24 hours of delivery. If you don't, the order is treated as received in good condition, the full amount is released to the seller, and no refund or claim will be accepted after that.
  • Defect, damage, shortage, tampering or wrong-product claims require a continuous unboxing video showing the package before opening through the product reveal.

7. Wrong or defective products

  • If a seller ships a wrong product or a defective product that does not match the photos or stated condition, the seller bears the full loss.
  • The buyer's unboxing video serves as proof. The seller will not receive payment or will be partially paid based on Brand Integer's assessment, and the buyer will be fully compensated.
  • To prevent disputes: sellers must clearly describe any flaws, defects, wear, stains or damage in the product description and include clear photos showing all imperfections.
  • Misrepresenting product condition is a violation and may result in account suspension.

8. Fraud protection

  • Brand Integer will use reasonable transaction controls to reduce fraudulent activity. No platform can guarantee that fraud is impossible, so users should provide accurate information and follow the transaction process.

9. Platform role

  • Brand Integer facilitates discovery, transaction coordination and payment release according to the stated process.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated:

Cancellation

  • Buyers can cancel free of charge any time before the product is shipped. Any amount paid is refunded in full.
  • Once shipped, an order cannot be cancelled; see returns below.
  • Brand Integer may cancel an order if the product is unavailable or the listing breaks our rules. You receive a full refund.

Refunds

  • Wrong, defective, damaged, incomplete or not-as-described product: full refund of the product price and platform fee, on review of your unboxing video. The seller bears the cost.
  • Lost or destroyed in transit: full refund to the buyer.
  • Change of mind: not eligible once shipped, unless the seller agrees.

How to raise a claim

  • WhatsApp us within 24 hours of delivery with your Order ID and unboxing video.
  • If no issue is reported within 24 hours, the order is treated as received in good condition, payment is released to the seller, and no refund or claim will be accepted after that.
  • We review claims within 3 working days.
  • Approved refunds are sent to your original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Privacy Policy

Last updated:

What we collect

  • Sellers: name, WhatsApp number, email, city, pincode, product details and photos.
  • Buyers: name, WhatsApp number, email, delivery address, city and pincode.

How we use it

  • To review listings, process orders, arrange delivery, handle payments, refunds and support, and prevent fraud.

Who we share it with

  • The seller receives the buyer's name and delivery address only, to ship the order.
  • Seller phone numbers are never shown to buyers, and buyer phone numbers are never shared with sellers.
  • Courier and payment partners receive only what they need to complete the order.
  • We do not sell your data.

Storage and your rights

  • Data is stored securely with Google services and kept only as long as needed for orders, accounting and legal requirements.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by contacting the Grievance Officer.
